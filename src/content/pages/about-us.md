## Where our humate comes from

Humusolver is made from humate mined in the San Juan Basin of northwest New Mexico, from the Fruitland Formation. These freshwater humus deposits formed from ancient plant material. We process it with a low-heat drying method into products that dissolve or blend easily, then ship them dry for easy freight and long storage.
