## What we collect

When you submit a quote request or question, we collect what you type into the form, such as your name, phone, email, farm name, location, acreage, and order details. We also record which page you sent it from.

## How we use it

We use it to answer your request: to call or email you, prepare a quote, and arrange shipping or pickup. Submissions are stored in a private spreadsheet that only Humusolver staff can access, and we get an email notification for each one.

## What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

## Cookies and analytics

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. [[OWNER CONFIRM: update this section if analytics is added at launch]]
