## How to use it

- **Rate:** 1–2 lb Humusolver-100 per acre in 1–3 gal of water.
- **Mixing:** Fill the tank partway, start agitation, then add the powder slowly before adding other products.
- **Compatibility:** Always jar-test with your exact starter blend before filling the tank. [[OWNER CONFIRM: known compatible starter blends]]

## Why at planting

Early root growth sets up the rest of the season. Humic substances are studied mainly for their effects on root development and nutrient uptake, so putting them near the seed at planting is a common choice. See the [research summaries](/research/) for what published studies have and haven't found.
