## What the research shows

- **+27% nitrogen use efficiency** and **+17% nitrogen uptake** on average across field studies, with the biggest gains in low-nitrogen, near-neutral soils (Ma et al., 2024 meta-analysis).
- **More yield, less nitrogen lost.** In a two-season winter wheat trial, urea with humic acid built in raised grain yield and fertilizer-nitrogen uptake and reduced nitrogen losses (Gao et al., 2022).

[See the studies](/research/)

## How growers use it

- **With liquid nitrogen (UAN):** Mix Humusolver-100 into the tank. Jar-test first.
- **With dry nitrogen:** Blend FS Granular with urea or other dry blends for broadcasting.
